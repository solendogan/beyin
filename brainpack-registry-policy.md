# Brainpack Registry Policy

## Core principle

Brainpack is a **local-first knowledge runtime**, not a content hosting platform.

Packs contain source references and configuration. All processing — transcripts, embeddings, indexes — happens on the user's own machine.

---

## What a pack may include

- YouTube video, playlist, and channel URLs
- Article and website URLs
- RSS feed URLs
- Metadata, tags, descriptions
- Author and contributor info

---

## What a pack must not include

- Full video or audio files you do not own
- Copied article bodies or transcripts without permission
- Paywalled content copied in full
- User-built embeddings or vector indexes
- Credentials, tokens, cookies, or session data
- Private or restricted content

**References are fine. Hosted copies of third-party content are not.**

---

## Removal requests

If you are a creator or rights holder and believe a pack references your content in a way that harms you, contact us. We will:

1. Review the request
2. Remove or edit the pack if appropriate
3. Respond promptly for credible complaints

Note: a pack that links to a public URL is generally not the same as hosting your content. We review each case individually.

---

## Packs we will remove

- Packs containing malware or harmful instructions
- Packs impersonating creators or brands
- Packs with deceptive descriptions
- Spam or low-quality submissions
- Packs referencing illegal content

---

## Contributor responsibility

By submitting a pack, you represent that:

- You have the right to reference the included sources
- You are not uploading infringing copied content
- The information is accurate to the best of your knowledge
